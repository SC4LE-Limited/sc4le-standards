# SC4LE LinkedIn Publishing Operating Practice

## 1. Purpose

This operating practice defines how the founder currently uses LinkedIn to:

- communicate approved SC4LE concepts
- build awareness of SC4LE Limited
- share founder insight and experience
- support learning and validation
- encourage meaningful professional discussion
- ensure AI-assisted publishing remains governed and transparent

This document records current founder practice.

It is governed organisational knowledge.

It does not define:

- SC4LE governance
- SC4LE architecture
- SC4LE operating model standards
- decision rights outside LinkedIn publishing

---

## 2. Scope

This operating practice applies to:

- the SC4LE Limited LinkedIn Company Page
- Scott Zebedee's LinkedIn personal profile when publishing SC4LE-related content
- AI-assisted creation of LinkedIn content

This operating practice does not govern:

- paid advertising
- direct messaging campaigns
- non-LinkedIn social media channels

---

## 3. Operating Principles

This operating practice is governed by the SC4LE Principles defined in:

`foundations/sc4le-principles.md`

All LinkedIn publishing activities, content creation, publishing decisions, review activities and AI-assisted workflows should be interpreted and applied through those principles.

Where this operating practice appears to conflict with the SC4LE Principles, the Principles take precedence.

---

## 4. Channel Roles

### 4.1 SC4LE Limited Company Page

The Company Page is used for:

- official SC4LE positions
- standards releases
- organisational announcements
- experiments and validation activities
- service and capability updates
- organisational content

Company content represents the organisation.

### 4.2 Scott Zebedee Personal Profile

The personal profile is used for:

- founder experience
- practical observations
- interpretation of approved content
- reflection and learning
- professional conversation

Personal content represents the founder's voice.

---

## 5. Relationship Between Channels

The Company Page establishes the organisational position.

The founder profile may:

- explain
- amplify
- interpret
- discuss

approved company content.

The founder profile must not redefine approved SC4LE concepts, terminology or governance.

Where both channels discuss the same topic, the Company Page remains the authoritative reference point.

---

## 6. Source Hierarchy

Content should normally be developed using the following source order:

1. Approved SC4LE Golden Source content
2. Active governed SC4LE content
3. Founder experience and direct observation
4. Verified external evidence
5. General industry observations

External sources may inform content.

They may not override approved SC4LE content.

---

## 7. Content Classification

LinkedIn content should be understood within one of the following categories.

### Approved Content

Content representing an approved SC4LE position.

### Proposed Content

Content under active development and not yet approved.

### Founder Experience

Personal observations, experiences and lessons learned.

### External Evidence

Information originating outside SC4LE.

### Experiment

Content intended to test, explore or validate ideas.

Founder, proposed and experimental content must not be represented as approved SC4LE positions.

---

## 8. Publishing Boundaries

The following are prohibited:

- inventing SC4LE concepts
- inventing SC4LE terminology
- presenting draft or proposed material as approved
- fabricating founder experience
- misrepresenting evidence
- publishing confidential information
- publishing private information without appropriate authority
- publishing misleading claims
- using LinkedIn performance as evidence to change SC4LE principles or governance

Publishing should favour usefulness, transparency and accuracy over engagement optimisation.

---

## 9. AI-Assisted Publishing

AI may assist with:

- research
- summarisation
- drafting
- editing
- content structuring
- idea generation

AI may not:

- approve content
- publish content autonomously
- invent founder experiences
- redefine SC4LE concepts
- override the source hierarchy
- alter approved SC4LE content without human review

Human accountability remains mandatory.

---

## 10. Review and Learning

LinkedIn activity may create:

- learning
- insight
- questions
- observations
- experiment outcomes

These may inform future development of SC4LE.

They do not automatically amend the SC4LE Golden Source.

Any proposed Golden Source changes must follow the applicable SC4LE governance process.

## 13. Review Criteria

This operating practice is operating correctly when:

- Company Page content remains aligned to approved SC4LE content
- Founder content remains consistent with the SC4LE Principles
- Founder experience remains distinguishable from approved standards
- Publishing remains sustainable
- AI assistance remains governed
- Learning informs improvement without governance drift

---

## 14. Review Cadence

Review this operating practice:

- quarterly
- after significant LinkedIn platform changes
- after major changes to the founder operating model
- after material changes to repository structure or governance

The objective of review is continuous improvement while maintaining clarity, simplicity and practical usability.
