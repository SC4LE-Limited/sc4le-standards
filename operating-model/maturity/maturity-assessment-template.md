This template is used to assess organisational capability against the SC4LE Maturity Model.

It provides a structured, evidence-based view of strengths, constraints, risks and recommended improvement actions. It may be used to support organisational diagnostics, capability development, governance review, enablement planning and withdrawal-readiness assessment.

The authoritative maturity standard is:

`operating-model/maturity/maturity-model.md`

This template applies the maturity philosophy, dimensions, levels and criteria defined by that standard. It must not redefine or introduce alternative maturity dimensions, maturity levels or assessment criteria.

Where this template conflicts with the authoritative maturity standard, the maturity standard takes precedence.

- governance records
- decision records

- customer or stakeholder feedback
